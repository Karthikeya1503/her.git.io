Your song goes here when you have chosen it.
1. Copy your licensed audio file here and name it our-song.mp3.
2. In src/config.js, change song.src from '' to '/music/our-song.mp3'.
3. Set song.title to your chosen title.
4. The opening button will enter the site AND call audio.play() from that tap.
No song is included or fetched by default. There is no fake playing state.
